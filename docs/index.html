What the platform guarantees

One record per student

Admission enquiry through to alumni, in a single history that never gets overwritten.

Attendance you can defend

Rotating QR codes with a geofence, plus the raw scan evidence kept for disputes.

Money that reconciles

Invoice-driven fees with an append-only ledger, so balances are derived, never typed in.

Isolated by the database

Row-Level Security on every table: one campus cannot read another, even by guessing an id.
